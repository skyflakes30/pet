# Animated story characters

Kat, Theia and Nythera now have original articulated SVG full-body sprites with independent hair, head, arm and leg motion, blinking and expression mouths. Kat walks around the ranch and adventure trail. Theia and Nythera appear as clearly labelled Heart echoes, so the farm remains a safe base rather than a physical gathering of enemies. Tap a character to open the current chapter scene.

The Story area includes all three actors and a Play chapter scene button. Each of the six existing opening chapter states has a five-beat scene with a highlighted speaker, changing expressions, character gestures, portrait dialogue and Back/Next controls. The final beat returns to the adventure; it does not complete objectives or grant rewards. Both saved choices use their existing ending dialogue. Scenes can be replayed freely.

Progression and rewards remain exclusively controlled by the existing ranch and battle engines. Saves, currency, pets and story flags are unchanged. No new boss encounters or later chapters are claimed. Existing portrait conversations remain available. Reduced-motion system preference disables the new movement; mobile maps retain horizontal touch panning and semantic tap targets.

Validation: 70 automated tests pass, syntax checks pass, production build copies 22 assets. New tests cover all three articulated rigs and four expressions, map cast placement, all chapter/branch scenes and non-mutating replay. Physical mobile hardware has not been tested.
